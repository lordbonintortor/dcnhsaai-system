Thank you for the opportunity to develop your association’s website and alumni registry. This quotation covers completion of the existing website design and implementation of the core online registration and officer review features described below.

## Scope of work

| Component | Deliverables |
| --- | --- |
| Public website | Responsive homepage, about/contact page, trustees and biographies, and pages for news, events, projects, management reports, and awardees, using client-supplied content and the existing design. |
| Alumni accounts | Graduate registration, email verification, sign-in/sign-out, password recovery, and an alumni dashboard. |
| Alumni profiles | Profile editing, profile photo upload, and application status and officer messages. |
| Alumni directory | Public search and batch filtering showing names and batch years; signed-in member profiles showing name, batch year, photo, and profession. Listing eligibility will be confirmed before development. |
| Officer dashboard | Authorized officer login, application search/filtering, approval, requests for information, rejection, reopening, graduation-reference recording, review history, and applicant resubmission. |
| Database and access | Shared database, production authentication, and server-enforced access controls for alumni, officers, and private review records. |
| Testing and launch | Functional and access checks, mobile/desktop layout checks, deployment to one production website, source-code handover, a brief administrator guide, and one remote training session of up to one hour. |

The website will be delivered in English. Initial content entry includes the existing supplied material and up to ten additional client-supplied entries across news, events, projects, reports, trustees, and awardees. Public content updates after handover are outside this package; a self-service content editor is not included.

## Project fee

**Total fixed development fee: ₱25,000.00**  
**Amount in words: Twenty-five thousand Philippine pesos only.**

This is a one-time development fee for the scope above. No recurring developer fee is included or required by this quotation. Domain registration, hosting, database/storage services, email delivery, and other paid third-party services are separate and payable by the client. Any paid service must be approved by the client before activation.

## Payment terms

**One payment of ₱25,000.00 upon completion and written client acceptance of the staging website, before production launch and source-code handover.** Payment is due within seven calendar days of the completion invoice. The project fee is the total client-payable development amount; any required tax documentation will be arranged before invoicing.

## Estimated delivery

**6–8 weeks** from written acceptance of this quotation and receipt of the complete approved content, requirements, and necessary service-account access.

The schedule assumes client feedback within five business days of each review. Delays in content, approvals, access, or third-party services may extend the delivery date.

## Revisions and acceptance

- Two consolidated rounds of design and content revisions within the agreed scope are included.
- The client will have ten business days to review the completed staging website and submit a consolidated list of issues against the agreed scope.
- Reproducible defects in agreed functionality will be corrected before final acceptance and do not count toward revision rounds.
- Production launch requires written client acceptance and full payment.
- Additional features or changes outside this scope require a separate written quotation and approval before work begins.

## Client responsibilities

The association will supply approved text, photographs, logos and permissions, final registration fields, authorized officer details, graduation-verification procedures, directory eligibility rules, and approved privacy and consent wording. Hosting and related service accounts will be registered under the client’s ownership.

Graduation eligibility will be assessed by authorized association officers. Automated verification against school records is outside the scope.

## Exclusions

The fee excludes a self-service content management system, CSV exports, advanced reports/analytics, existing-data imports or migration, applicant proof-document uploads, online payments or donation processing, SMS, bulk messaging, chat, job boards, mobile applications, and integrations with external school systems. Copywriting, brand design, ongoing content updates, and ongoing maintenance are also excluded. These services may be quoted separately.

## Warranty and handover

Includes **30 calendar days of defect correction after launch** for reproducible issues in the agreed delivered functionality. New features, content changes, third-party service issues, and changes made by others are outside this warranty.

Source code, project documentation, and administrative access will be handed over after full payment. Third-party components remain subject to their respective licenses.
